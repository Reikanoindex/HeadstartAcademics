Who we are

Science made meaningful

Headstart Academics was created to help students build strong scientific understanding through effective teaching and practical application. Our approach brings together experience with both Thailand and Singapore science curricula.

Lessons are offered in English and Thai so students can learn in the language that supports them best. Laboratory activities turn abstract ideas into something students can see, test and remember.

“Understand through practical application. Succeed through effective learning.”

— Headstart Academics
